        AquaCare phone app

        A working mock-up of the proposed phone app, at real phone size. Open this page on a phone to use it full screen.

        • It runs its own copy of the AquaCare simulation in this browser. Press Start, then use the speed button to move time faster.
        • At a feed time the app shows the 10-minute countdown. Only the button at the tank can confirm a hand feed; the red Tank button at the top of the app stands in for it.
        • Under Settings, disconnect Wi-Fi to see the app go out of date while the controller keeps feeding.
